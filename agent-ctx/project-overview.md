# Landing Page Project — LUMINA Majestic

## Overview
LUMINA (لومينا) is a luxury brand specializing in illuminated porcelain art panels that merge art, decor, and LED lighting into a single masterpiece. The panels feature fine artistic details with crystal stone embellishments and embedded LED lighting, crafted from premium porcelain. The brand targets luxury homes, villas, hotels, and interior designers primarily in **Qatar** and the **Gulf region**.

**Brand Tagline:** متخصصين في اللوحات الفنية المضيئة (Specialists in Illuminated Art Panels)

## The Core Problem & Solution
- **Problem:** Art panels are treated as generic, replaceable decor items with no uniqueness or personality. Clients seeking luxury wall art that truly reflects their identity and space have no premium options that combine visual art with functional ambient lighting.
- **Solution:** LUMINA redefines the concept of art panels by merging contemporary art with smart LED illumination and high craftsmanship. Each piece is a limited edition made from premium porcelain, carefully inlaid with crystal and selected stones, granting every space an unforgettable visual presence.

## Conversion Goal
The primary action is to drive visitors to **contact via WhatsApp** for inquiries and purchases. Secondary actions include visiting the Instagram page for portfolio/social proof and the main website for the full catalog.

## Landing Page Sections
1. **Hero Section:** Full-screen dark luxury hero with animated headline, glowing subtitle, and dual CTAs (WhatsApp + Explore).
2. **About / Brand Story:** Elegant narrative about LUMINA's philosophy — redefining art panels, first in Qatar.
3. **Features / USP:** 3-4 key differentiators with icons (Porcelain Craft, LED Illumination, Crystal Inlay, Limited Edition).
4. **Product Showcase:** Grid of 4-6 featured products with images, names, and prices.
5. **Categories:** Visual category cards (Animals, Luxury Cars, Islamic Art, Skyline).
6. **Social Proof:** Instagram feed or testimonials with WhatsApp CTA.
7. **Final CTA:** Strong WhatsApp call-to-action with phone number and Instagram link.
8. **Footer:** Brand logo, social links, copyright, quick links.

## Scope
### In Scope
- Single page responsive layout (Arabic RTL).
- Mobile-first design.
- Fast loading (Lighthouse > 90).
- WhatsApp button as primary CTA throughout.
- Instagram and website links in footer/header.
- RTL (Right-to-Left) layout for Arabic content.
- Smooth scroll animations and luxury micro-interactions.
- Dark luxury theme with gold accents and LED glow effects.

### Out of Scope
- Multi-page website or e-commerce functionality.
- User authentication or cart system.
- Complex CMS or Dashboard.
- English language version (Arabic only for now).
- Backend API or database.
